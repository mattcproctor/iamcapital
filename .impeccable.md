# Design Context

The site reproduces the original iamcapital.com design (Brizy, as live until October 2026). It is not a redesign:
keep new work consistent with it.

- **Source of truth**: the archived pages (Wayback Machine, May–July 2026). Fonts, sizes, colours and spacing in
  `src/styles/global.css` and the page styles were measured from those pages.
- **Type**: Overpass throughout (headings 700/600, body 16px/1.9); Lato for counters and fund/property fact tables;
  Source Sans for the history timeline.
- **Colour**: navy #19375F, black text, greys #7F7F7F / #8E8E8E, light grey sections #F2F2F2, white.
- **Patterns**: photo banner with white title and 4px rule; section titles with a 100–117px rule; four-across
  document cards (title, month, navy bar, READ MORE); fund cards with "View details" accordions; team cards with a
  biography pop-up; navy footer in four columns.
- **Mobile**: measured from the original at 390px: H1 32px (banner titles 36px), H2 28px, H3 22px, H4 22px, 56px
  counters, banners ~200px, 78px header with the in-page sub-menu kept visible under the logo, bold document titles.
- **Icons**: the original's Font Awesome icons are the same Font Awesome Free glyphs; its Nucleo icons (licensed only
  inside Brizy) are replaced by the closest Font Awesome Free (solid) or Lucide (outline) icon.
- **Deliberate differences**: documents paginate in the browser instead of separate /bpage/2/ URLs; Ever Arbib appears
  once (the old Team page listed him twice with different titles).
